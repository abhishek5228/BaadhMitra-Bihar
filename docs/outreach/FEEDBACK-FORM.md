# Feedback form (ten questions, about 10 minutes)

Run the demo (`docs/DEMO-SCRIPT.md`) and ask these. Score 1 (no) to 5 (yes), then one line of why. Write down what they say, including the unflattering.

1. A dispatcher reading the queue would trust its ranking. (1-5)
2. The ranking matches who you would send help to first. Which item would you move? (1-5 + note)
3. The evidence panel shows enough to decide. What is missing? (1-5 + note)
4. The overdue clock timings (15/30 min urgent, 30/60 normal, 60/120 silent crew) fit your practice. What would you change? (1-5 + numbers)
5. Rescue crews could use the phone page in the field. What would stop them? (1-5 + note)
6. Citizens could use the report page. What would confuse them? (1-5 + note)
7. The privacy handling (no phone numbers stored, audit log) is acceptable. What worries you? (1-5 + note)
8. Which part is least useful, or should be removed?
9. What is the one thing it must do that it does not?
10. Would you try it in a shadow pilot next to your current process? What would you need first? (yes / maybe / no + reason)

Also record: their role (a general description is enough), the date, how long the demo ran, and **whether you may name them or their organisation**. If the answer is no or not stated, publish anonymously.
